## Site Skins

Wondering why my AO3 screenshots look different than yours? Site Skins are why.
Site skins are a fantastic way to change the look of AO3 on both desktop and
mobile. The site skins below are my absolute faves. I've used both for years,
and I highly recommend them!

- [Rosé Pine Site Skins ](https://blackbatcats.tumblr.com/post/793204650133307392/release-ros%C3%A9-pine-site-skins)
- [Tide: a flat and clean skin for Ao3](https://archiveofourown.org/works/32660914)

## Userscripts

### Everyone

- [AO3 Fix Paragraph Spacing](https://update.greasyfork.org/scripts/389365/AO3%20Fix%20Paragraph%20Spacing.user.js)
  - Removes excessive spacing between paragraphs on AO3.
- [AO3 Menu Items](https://update.greasyfork.org/scripts/18484/ao3%20menu%20items.user.js)
  - Add more options to dropdown.
- [AO3: Reorder Tags with Drag & Drop](https://update.greasyfork.org/scripts/474458/AO3%3A%20Reorder%20Tags%20with%20Drag%20%20Drop.user.js)
  - Drag & drop tags into the order you'd like before posting. Copy and reorder
    tags after posting, if needed.
- [AO3:Comment Formatting and Preview](https://update.greasyfork.org/scripts/484002/AO3%3A%20Comment%20Formatting%20and%20Preview.user.js)
  - Adds buttons to insert HTML formatting, and shows a live preview box of how
    the comment will look.
- [ao3 rekudos converter](https://update.greasyfork.org/scripts/427421/ao3%20rekudos%20converter.user.js)
  - automatically comment on a fic if you've left kudos before.

### Exchange/Event Mods

- [AO3 Exchange requests' names link to their profiles](https://update.greasyfork.org/scripts/429035/AO3%20Exchange%20requests%27%20names%20link%20to%20their%20profiles.user.js)
  - Adds linked names to requests.
- [AO3 Hide Drafts in Collection](https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/583687-ao3-hide-drafts-in-collection)
  - Adds a toggle button on the Manage Items page of collections to Hide/Show
    Drafts to make reviewing and approving works easier.
- [Add Owned Tagset to AO3 Menus](https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/583684-add-owned-tagset-to-ao3-menus)
  - Adds a link to tag sets you own to your own user pages. Should work with
    most site skins as long as they do not hide the user greeting ("Hi,
    [username]!").
